# External source and county-directory review — October 8, 2026

Completed the directory follow-up authorized by the owner. Payment testing was excluded at the owner’s request.

## What changed

- Filled 116 missing office entries across 91 counties. All 373 supported counties now have an entry for each of the five office categories. An entry can be a contact page or official directory; this does not mean every county has a current property auction.
- Recorded completed page-purpose reviews for 77 new entries. The other 39 retain a blank verification date.
- Labeled office contact/directory pages separately from actual tax-sale information. Generic tax-office fallback links no longer promise auction dates or bidding rules.
- Shared URLs now preserve every office label and purpose rather than hiding the later office cards.
- Corrected the misleading Crosby appraisal-search “tax sale” description and the broad Wayne county homepage fallbacks. Used official destination URLs for Pima, Alameda, Mendocino, Santa Clara and Wichita redirects.
- Replaced the California FTB search link with its actual like-kind exchange reporting explanation. Clarified that reporting generally continues until California deferred gain or loss is recognized, including after a further exchange.
- Replaced the unavailable Utah bill page with the Utah House’s official explanation of the 2026 income-tax-rate change.
- Included the tax lesson in the Learn review ledger so later edits require a new content review.

## Scope and limitations

- Attempted to retrieve all 142 source records that lacked verification dates at the start of this pass: 56 returned page content and 86 did not return usable content.
- Retrieval alone does not verify a financial/legal proposition. A verification date was recorded only where the office/page purpose or specific changed claim was reviewed. Other dates remain blank, including some accessible legal references that still require a complete claim-by-claim review.
- Researched missing entries against official county, sheriff, state property-tax and public-safety pages. Indexed official links can be included without a completed retrieval; those remain explicitly undated.
- A 403, 502, restricted-URL or research-tool error does not establish that the public link is broken. It means the reviewer could not verify that page through the available reader. JavaScript/search portals also require a separate interaction check.
- No payment, bidding, login, private records, document purchase or license-holder search was submitted. This pass did not retest all previously dated sources.
