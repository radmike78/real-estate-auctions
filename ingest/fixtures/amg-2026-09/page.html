Notice of Sale

City of Henderson - October 27, 2026


 *The City of Las Vegas, the City of Reno, the City of Henderson, and Clark County have updated the procedure for bidder cards.  Only the name on the picture ID provided at registration will be used as the recorded name on the certificate of sale.

                                        

 

Generally, all sales are conducted the last week of the sale month, unless noted. The listing will be posted here and published in the paper approx. three weeks prior to the sale date. Registration for the sale will begin approx. 30 to 60 minutes prior to the sale time. All registrations must be completed in person at the sale prior to the sale time. No registrations will be allowed after the sale start time.
