`index.json` mixes two kinds of records, in the exact shape of https://www.irsauctions.gov/index.json:

- The first 8 are **real** ads from the live index, retrieved 2026-09-26 (none in Nevada). The notice
  text after each property address is removed because it names the taxpayer.
- The rest are **made up** (titles start with "MADE UP"; parcels in book 999 don't exist). They cover
  every case: a Clark County seizure sale, a court-ordered sealed-bid sale, a vehicle, a canceled
  sale, a sale with no date yet, ads in Reno and Pahrump (Nevada, not Clark County), land in Mesa,
  Arizona (with a phone number that must not be read as a parcel), and a California town we can't
  place in a county.
